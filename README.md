"The World in One Image" is a front-page poster that sums up the world news of Thursday, 19 November 2026, in a single 1024×1024 image. Five stories, each kept to the facts as reported that day, fill the page: the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit opening in Shenzhen, Israel's ground operation in central Gaza, clashes between Ethiopia and Eritrea threatening full-scale war, governments warning that a Java flaw leaves computer systems open to hackers, and a magnitude 5.7 earthquake jolting Kumamoto Prefecture in Japan. The date is printed large across the masthead, every story pairs a short headline and plain summary with a drawn illustration, and all text is rendered as crisp HTML text — no words inside generated images.
